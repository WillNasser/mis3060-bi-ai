Question: "For a quarterly portfolio review, how do loan count, default rate, and dollar exposure compare across Wildcat's five loan purposes (Auto, Personal, Home Improvement, Education, Business) — and which purpose would a portfolio committee likely flag as the highest-risk concentration?"

But Business, despite a middling 3.35% default rate, has the largest dollar amount currently in default ($3.16M) simply due to loan size. Claude noted a portfolio committee would likely flag Business as the top concentration-risk item (single-purpose exposure over half the book) while flagging Home Improvement separately as the credit-quality outlier (highest default rate).
