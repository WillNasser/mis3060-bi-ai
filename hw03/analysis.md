# Do executive changes precede or follow earnings announcements?

In my data, executive changes lean toward coming before an earnings announcement. 21 of the 28 events (11 of the 16 distinct 8-K filings) were filed before the nearest earnings release, 5 events (4 filings) after it, and 2 events (one Walmart filing, 6 days before earnings) landed in the same week. The pattern is fairly consistent for JPMorgan (all 4 events before), Walmart (8 of 10 before, the other 2 same-week), Apple (4 of 5 before) and NVIDIA (4 of 6 before), but Microsoft is mixed, with two of its three events coming after earnings. Most "before" events were filed roughly 2 to 6 weeks ahead of the release, not right up against it. This should be read cautiously, though: with only five companies, about a year of data and 16 independent filings, the lean could easily be chance, and because each event is matched to whichever earnings date is closest, events in the middle of a quarter can fall on either side.
